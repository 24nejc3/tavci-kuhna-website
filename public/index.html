just outside Bled

Plant-based food, ten minutes from Lake Bled.

Lesce is the quiet next-door neighbour to Bled. Free parking, no tourist crush, alpine light, wooden interior. The kind of stop you double back to find.

Whether you've come down from Vintgar Gorge, off the Bled boat, or finished a ride along the Sava — we're the warm, generous lunch waiting at the end.

the cake counter

Snickers cake, Oreo cake, vegan cream cake.

The dessert program isn't a side thought. Customers come for cakes alone, and they take slices home in boxes. Even the Bled-style kremna rezina — that classic regional cream cake — comes plant-based here.

The cakes are generous. The reviewers name them by name. There's almost always something new on the counter.

"We took two slices of Snickers cake away to have with a coffee later. The taste was really good. Would love the recipe." — Tripadvisor review

about us

A small alpine deli that takes its food seriously.

Tavči Kuhna is a vegan restaurant, café, and deli in Lesce — close enough to Bled to feel its energy, far enough to keep the calm. The kitchen makes plant-based food that holds its own beside any traditional Slovenian plate: generous burgers, homemade falafel, gyros, fresh salads, and a dessert counter that has its own following.

Open every day except Tuesday. Free parking. Wolt delivery. And almost always, a slice of Snickers cake left on the counter.

Together with Ayatana deli

Right next door, Ayatana ferments kombucha, water kefir, and a colourful collection of plant-based goodies. We pour their kombucha straight from the tap. You can also take some home from our deli shelf.
