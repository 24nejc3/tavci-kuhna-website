One address, two crafts.

Tavči Kuhna cooks. Ayatana ferments. We're next-door neighbours in Lesce, sharing a parking lot, which means the kombucha in your glass didn't travel across Europe — it travelled across the car park.

Ayatana works the traditional way: organic ingredients, live cultures, and slow, patient fermentation. No shortcuts, no syrupy imitations. Just fermented tea the way it's supposed to taste — lightly sour, naturally fizzy, genuinely refreshing.

new to kombucha?

Fermented tea, nothing weird about it

People have been brewing it for centuries. Here's the short version.

1.

It starts as tea

Real brewed tea, organic sugar, and a living culture. That's the whole ingredient list at the start.

2.

Time does the work

The culture slowly eats the sugar and transforms the tea — building gentle fizz, bright acidity, and depth of flavour along the way.

3.

You drink it cold

The result is lightly tart, naturally sparkling, and far less sweet than soda. Refreshing on its own, brilliant with food.
