the beans

Roasted beneath Stol, brewed in Lesce.

Our coffee comes from Kolektiv22, a Slovenian specialty roastery working in the shadow of Mt. Stol — the same mountain you can see from our corner of the valley. Short supply chain, fresh roast dates, and a roaster we can call by name.

Because the whole café is plant-based, every coffee comes with plant milk as standard. Oat flat white, soy cappuccino, an honest black espresso — no surcharge, no asking twice.

the obvious pairing

Coffee here comes with a decision to make.

The cake counter sits right next to the coffee machine — we're not sorry. A cappuccino with a slice of our vegan Bled cream cake, or an espresso beside a glazed donut, is the way most afternoons here go.

Every dessert is baked in our own kitchen, dairy-free and egg-free. Even the espresso's little sidekick is homemade.

coffee near Bled

Skip the lakeside queue — drive ten minutes.

Looking for specialty coffee near Lake Bled? Lesce is the quiet next-door neighbour: free parking right at the door, no tourist crush, and a warm wooden café where you can actually hear your own conversation.

By car

10 minutes from Bled

Follow Alpska cesta into Lesce. We're at number 43C, with free parking right out front — a rare thing this close to the lake.

By train

Lesce–Bled station

The railway station is a short walk away. If you're touring Slovenia by train, we're one of the easiest specialty coffee stops in the Julian Alps.

By bike

Along the Sava

Finish a ride through the valley with a flat white and a slice of cake. Cyclists are regulars here — you'll be in good company.

good to know

Questions, answered

Where can I get specialty coffee near Lake Bled?

Right here — Tavči Kuhna × Ayatana deli in Lesce, a ten-minute drive from Lake Bled. We serve Kolektiv22 specialty beans, roasted locally beneath Mt. Stol, with free parking at the door.

Do you have oat milk?

Yes — and soy, and more. The whole café is plant-based, so every coffee is made with plant milk as standard. No surcharge, no asking twice.

Can I work from the café?

Please do. There's wifi, a cosy mezzanine, sofa corners, and no loud music. Order a coffee, open the laptop, stay for the daily lunch.

When are you open?

Monday to Sunday, 11:00–20:00. Tuesdays we're closed — the coffee machine rests too.
